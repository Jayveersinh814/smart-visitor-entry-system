What is GatePass QR?
GatePass QR is a web-based visitor management system that replaces outdated paper registers with a smart, QR-driven digital workflow. When a visitor is registered, the system generates a unique QR code that holds all visit details — purpose, host, time window, and authorization status. Guards simply scan this QR at the gate to instantly verify access, log entry/exit times, and flag anomalies in real time.

Simple Process

How it works

From visitor arrival to verified exit in just a few steps.

1
Register Visitor
Receptionist fills in visitor details, purpose of visit, host name, and time window.
2
Approval Review
The designated approver receives the request and approves, holds, or rejects it.
3
QR Generated
On approval, a unique QR pass is issued and shared with the visitor instantly.
4
Gate Scan
Security guard scans the QR at entry — the system verifies validity and logs the time.
5
Exit Logged
On departure, a second scan records exit time, completing the visitor's full record.
